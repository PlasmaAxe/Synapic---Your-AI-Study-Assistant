# 🧠 Synapic

### AI-powered study assistant built to make studying faster and smarter.

🌐 **[Try Synapic](https://synapicai.vercel.app)**

Synapic is a full-stack AI study platform that turns uploaded notes and documents into useful study material in seconds.

Instead of spending hours creating study resources manually, Synapic uses AI to generate **flashcards, quizzes, summaries and personalised study plans** from your own material.

---

## ✨ Features

📝 **AI Summaries**  
Turn long notes and documents into concise, easy-to-understand summaries.

🧠 **Flashcards**  
Automatically generate flashcards from your study material.

❓ **AI Quizzes**  
Create quizzes based on your notes and test your understanding.

📅 **Study Plans**  
Generate personalised study plans based on your subjects and learning goals.

🔐 **User Accounts**  
Secure authentication with personalised data and persistent study history.

🤖 **AI Study Chat**  
Currently developing RAG functionality to allow users to chat directly with their uploaded notes.

## 💡 Why I Built It

I wanted to build something that solved a problem I actually experience as a student.

A lot of time can be spent preparing study material before actually getting to the studying. Synapic aims to automate some of that preparation while keeping the student in control of how they learn.

Building Synapic has also given me the opportunity to work with **AI/LLM APIs, full-stack development, databases, authentication, deployment and cloud infrastructure** in a real application.
